What is GPU FinOps & Token Chargeback? ​

Welcome! If you are new to AI infrastructure, FinOps, or Kubernetes cost tracking, this guide explains everything from first principles.

1. The Core Problem: Why is AI Compute So Expensive? ​

When an enterprise or startup builds AI applications (like chatbots, code generators, or image models), they rely on NVIDIA GPUs (graphics processing units) and LLM inference APIs (like vLLM, Ollama, OpenAI).

Unlike traditional web servers (which cost a few cents a day), GPUs cost up to $4.00 per hour PER GPU. An 8-GPU node running an AI model costs $2,300+ per month.

Traditional CPU Web ServerNVIDIA H100 / A100 GPU Node
~$30.00 / month~$2,500.00 to $20,000.00 / month

When multiple software teams share the same Kubernetes cluster, the monthly cloud bill arrives as one huge sum (e.g. $50,000 / month).

The Problem: Platform engineering cannot tell:

  • Who spent the money?
  • Which department (NLP team, Computer Vision team, Finance AI team) used how many GPU-hours?
  • Which team processed how many LLM tokens?

2. What is FinOps? ​

FinOps stands for Financial Operations (or Cloud Financial Management).

It is the discipline of bringing financial accountability to the variable spend model of cloud and AI infrastructure. Instead of finding out your cloud bill at the end of the month, FinOps provides:

  1. Visibility: Real-time tracking of who is spending what.
  2. Attribution: Mapping costs directly to teams, products, or departments.
  3. Optimization: Identifying idle GPUs and oversized workloads.

3. What is Chargeback vs. Showback? ​

TermWhat It MeansReal-World Analogy
ShowbackGenerating a report that shows each team how much money their AI models spent, without actually billing their department budget.Sending a mock utility bill to each roommate to make them aware of their electricity usage.
ChargebackActually deducting or billing the cost from the specific team's internal department budget.Splitting and charging the utility bill based on who ran the air conditioner.

Slicemeter generates the clean data, CSVs, tables, and PDF reports required to do both Showback and Chargeback.

4. What is Token Chargeback? ​

Large Language Models (LLMs) do not process text in words: they process text in Tokens (roughly 4 characters of text).

When multiple teams send requests to a shared internal AI endpoint (e.g., http://ollama-service.internal or http://vllm-service.internal):

  • Team A might generate 1,000,000 prompt tokens (e.g., summarizing large PDF documents).
  • Team B might generate 50,000 prompt tokens (e.g., quick search queries).

Without Slicemeter, both teams appear to be hitting the same URL. Slicemeter acts as a lightweight observer or proxy to record exactly how many tokens were generated by each namespace and team.
